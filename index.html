About Faseeh Academy

Dedicated To Meaningful Quran Learning

Faseeh Academy is an online Quran learning platform dedicated to helping Muslims around the world recite and memorize the Quran correctly. Our mission is to make Quran learning accessible to non-Arabic speakers through structured lessons, qualified teachers, and personalized guidance.

We focus on beginners learning Quran recitation, non-Arabic speakers, and students with basic recitation who want to improve and master Tajweed. We do not specialize in advanced ijazah programs or certification tracks for already highly proficient reciters.

Although the academy was established in 2025, our teachers collectively bring over 10 years of experience teaching Quran and Arabic to non-Arabic speakers.

Teaching is done in Riwayat Hafs 'An Asim, the most widely recited narration of the Quran worldwide.

  • Correct pronunciation (Tajweed)
  • Structured memorization (Hifz)
  • Confidence in recitation
  • Personal mentorship
